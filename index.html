1.Nayakan

Introduction

Nayakan is a 1987 Tamil crime drama directed by Mani Ratnam. The film stars Kamal Haasan in one of his finest performances and is inspired by the life of Mumbai underworld figure Varadarajan Mudaliar. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest Indian films ever made.

Story

The film follows the journey of Velu Naicker, who flees to Mumbai after his father's death. Over time, he rises as a powerful leader who protects the poor while battling criminals and corruption. The story explores his transformation from an innocent boy into a feared don.

Why It Is Special

Powerful storytelling Outstanding acting by Kamal Haasan Memorable music by Ilaiyaraaja Realistic portrayal of crime and justice

Conclusion

Nayakan remains a timeless masterpiece because of its emotional depth, unforgettable performances, and realistic storytelling.

2.Baashha

Introduction

Baashha is a 1995 action film directed by Suresh Krissna and starring Rajinikanth. It is one of the most iconic Tamil commercial films ever made.

Story

Manickam is a humble auto driver who hides his mysterious past. When his family is threatened, he reveals that he was once the powerful gangster Baashha, feared by criminals across Mumbai.

Why It Is Special

Rajinikanth's legendary screen presence Memorable dialogues Mass action sequences Strong family emotions

Conclusion

Baashha redefined the action genre in Tamil cinema and remains one of Rajinikanth's greatest films.

3.Thalapathi

Introduction

Thalapathi is a 1991 crime drama directed by Mani Ratnam starring Rajinikanth and Mammootty. The story is loosely inspired by the friendship between Karna and Duryodhana from the Mahabharata.

Story

Surya grows up without knowing his family and later becomes close friends with a local leader named Devaraj. Together they fight injustice while facing personal and emotional conflicts.

Why It Is Special

Strong friendship theme Excellent performances Beautiful cinematography Music by Ilaiyaraaja

Conclusion

Thalapathi is remembered for its emotional storytelling, powerful characters, and timeless music.

4.Vikram

Introduction

Vikram is a 2022 action thriller directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj. The film stars Kamal Haasan, Vijay Sethupathi, and Fahadh Faasil and is part of the Lokesh Cinematic Universe (LCU).

Story

A special agent investigates a series of mysterious murders while uncovering a dangerous criminal network involved in drug trafficking. The mission becomes personal as shocking secrets are revealed.

Why It Is Special

Fast-paced action Strong performances Stylish direction High-quality visual effects and music

Conclusion

Vikram is celebrated for its gripping screenplay, action sequences, and modern filmmaking techniques.

5.Jai Bhim

Introduction

Jai Bhim is a 2021 legal drama directed by T. J. Gnanavel and starring Suriya. The film is based on true events and highlights issues of social justice and human rights.

Story

A tribal man is falsely arrested and goes missing in police custody. His wife seeks justice with the help of an honest lawyer who fights against corruption and discrimination.

Why It Is Special

Inspired by real events Powerful social message Outstanding performances Emotionally impactful courtroom scenes

Conclusion

Jai Bhim is more than a film—it is a powerful reminder of the importance of justice, equality, and the rule of law. It continues to inspire audiences with its meaningful message.
