1.Nayakan

Introduction

Nayakan is a 1987 Tamil crime drama directed by Mani Ratnam. The film stars Kamal Haasan in one of his finest performances and is inspired by the life of Mumbai underworld figure Varadarajan Mudaliar. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest Indian films ever made.

Story

The film follows the journey of Velu Naicker, who flees to Mumbai after his father's death. Over time, he rises as a powerful leader who protects the poor while battling criminals and corruption. The story explores his transformation from an innocent boy into a feared don.

Why It Is Special

Powerful storytelling Outstanding acting by Kamal Haasan Memorable music by Ilaiyaraaja Realistic portrayal of crime and justice

Conclusion

Nayakan remains a timeless masterpiece because of its emotional depth, unforgettable performances, and realistic storytelling.

2.Baashha

Introduction

Baashha is a 1995 action film directed by Suresh Krissna and starring Rajinikanth. It is one of the most iconic Tamil commercial films ever made.

Story

Manickam is a humble auto driver who hides his mysterious past. When his family is threatened, he reveals that he was once the powerful gangster Baashha, feared by criminals across Mumbai.

Why It Is Special

Rajinikanth's legendary screen presence Memorable dialogues Mass action sequences Strong family emotions

Conclusion

Baashha redefined the action genre in Tamil cinema and remains one of Rajinikanth's greatest films.
